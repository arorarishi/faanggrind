Why FAANGrind

## The smarter way to prepare.

One focused workspace to find, solve, revise and track what actually matters for your target companies, from the coding round to the specialist round.

### Company-wise lists

Every problem tagged with who asked it and where it sat in that company's list, so you solve the most-reported ones first.

### Fresh, dated lists

Lists are rebuilt from interview reports in the last 30 or 90 days, not from a decade-old question bank.

### A roadmap for every track

Twenty DSA topics in learning order, system design fundamentals, and step-by-step roadmaps for GenAI and DevOps with quizzes.

### Streaks, no account

A daily streak that lives in your browser. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to sync.

### Personal notes

Markdown notes on every problem and lesson, autosaved locally. Export them as JSON whenever you want.

### Command palette

Press ⌘K anywhere to jump to any problem, company, topic or roadmap without lifting your hands off the keyboard.

Reported order

### Built for signal. Designed for speed.

Every company list keeps the order in which questions were reported. Solve the top 20 for your target company first and you have covered most of what you will see.
