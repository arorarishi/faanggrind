## What we know

Example 1

```
Input: stones = [2,7,4,1,8,1]
Output: 1
```

8 and 7 -> 1; 4 and 2 -> 2; 2 and 1 -> 1; 1 and 1 -> 0; one stone of weight 1 is left.

Example 2

```
Input: stones = [1]
Output: 1
```

## Constraints

* •

  `1 <= stones.length <= 30`
* •

  `1 <= stones[i] <= 1000`
