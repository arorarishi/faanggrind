## What we know

Example 1

```
Input: nums = [3,1,5,8]
Output: 167
```

Burst 1, 5, 3, 8 in that order: 3\*1\*5 + 3\*5\*8 + 1\*3\*8 + 1\*8\*1 = 167.

```
Input: nums = [1,5]
Output: 10
```
