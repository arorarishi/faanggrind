## What we know

Example 1

```
Input: ["TicTacToe","move","move","move","move","move","move","move"]
[[3],[0,0,1],[0,2,2],[2,2,1],[1,1,2],[2,0,1],[1,0,2],[2,1,1]]
Output: [null,0,0,0,0,0,0,1]
```

Player 1 completes the bottom row on the final move.

## Constraints

* •

  `2 <= n <= 100`
* •

  `player` is 1 or 2.
* •

  `0 <= row, col < n`
* •

  Every move is valid and made on an empty cell.
